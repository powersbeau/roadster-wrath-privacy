Roadster Wrath Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

Roadster Wrath is an arcade shooting game made by PowersPack Production. This policy explains what data the game handles and what happens to it. In short: the game does not collect any personal information. Nothing you do in the game leaves your device.

Data stored on your device

The game saves a small amount of information only on your phone, in the app's private storage:

  • Your high score
  • Your sound setting (on or off)

We have no servers that receive this data, and the game has no user accounts, sign-in or online leaderboards. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, this information is permanently deleted.

Internet access

Roadster Wrath plays completely offline. The app includes Android's standard internet permission because it is part of the framework the game is built with, but the game itself does not send or receive any data.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no advertising IDs
  • No in-app purchases
  • No analytics or tracking tools
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or file access
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone

Children

Roadster Wrath is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
